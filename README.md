# Little Rhythm Board

This is my little rhythm board. It is a row of numbered, colorful pads that you can tap, and it has a beat sequencer that loops by itself. Now you can change how fast it plays! One day it will play beside the scrape board.

How to play: tap or click a pad to hear a soft tap. Each pad has its own note, low on the left and higher on the right. Under the pads is a grid of six rows, one per pad, matching the pad colors from top to bottom. Each column is a step. Tap squares to switch them on, then press Play beat and the pattern loops. A white glow and the little bar under the grid show which step is playing. Press Stop t

Drag the Speed slider to make the beat slower or faster, even while it plays.

The sounds are made right in the browser with the Web Audio API. A scrape-like pattern is coming in the last step!
