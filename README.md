# Little Rhythm Board

This is my little rhythm board. It is a row of colorful pads that you can tap, and it will one day play beats beside the scrape board.

How to play: tap or click a pad to hear a soft tap and watch it light up. Each pad has its own note, low on the left and higher on the right. You can also tab to a pad and press Enter or Space.

The sounds are made right in the browser with the Web Audio API. A looping beat and a speed knob are coming in the next steps!
