# Little Rhythm Board

This is my little rhythm board. It is a row of numbered, colorful pads you can tap, with a beat sequencer that loops by itself, and now a scrape-like pattern to play along with!

How to play: tap or click a pad to hear a soft tap. Each pad has its own note, low on the left and higher on the right. Under the pads is a grid of six rows, one per pad, matching the pad colors. Each column is a step. Tap squares to switch them on, then press Play beat and the pattern loops. A white glow and the little bar under the grid show the current step.

Press the Scrape pattern button to add a scratchy, swishy rhythm on top, like dragging a pebble across wood. Drag the Speed slider to go slower or faster, even while it plays.

All the sounds are made right in the browser with the Web Audio API.
