# Little Rhythm Board

This is my little rhythm board. It is a row of colorful pads that you can tap, and it will one day play beats beside the scrape board.

How to play: just tap or click a pad and watch it light up. You can also tab to a pad and press Enter or Space.

Right now it only has the pads. Sounds, a looping beat and a speed knob are coming in the next steps!
