# Little Rhythm Board

This is my little rhythm board. It is a row of colorful pads that you can tap, and now it has a beat sequencer that loops by itself. One day it will play beside the scrape board.

How to play: tap or click a pad to hear a soft tap. Each pad has its own note, low on the left and higher on the right. Under the pads is a grid: each row matches a pad, each column is a step. Tap squares to switch them on, then press Play beat and the pattern loops. Press Stop to stop it.

The sounds are made right in the browser with the Web Audio API. A speed knob and a scrape-like pattern are coming in the next steps!
